      04 · Court
      
  
      The court reads evidence, not excuses
      
  
      The law: the agent may explain itself, but it cannot approve or acquit itself. No Guard method even takes its explanation as input. Findings come from the program it wrote and the checks it met. A verdict cannot reverse a payment.
      
  
      
  
      

      


      
  
      05 · Evolve
      
  
      The law changes only as a new version
      
  
      This is what a reviewer approves: added and removed lines. The new mandate gets a new hash and version. On NEAR, Guard refuses to accept any intent the agent registered under an older version.
